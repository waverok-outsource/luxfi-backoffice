## Purpose

Track and manage all financial transactions: asset sales, purchases, loan repayments, loan disbursements, wallet deposits, and interest settlements. Six history tabs plus aggregate metrics.

### Tabs (6)

#### 1. Asset Sales History (default)
**Table columns:** Transaction ID, Asset ID, Asset Name, Transaction Value, Party Name, Date, Payment Method, Payment Channel, Party ID, Party Email, Approver, Status (badge: completed/pending/failed)
**Data source:** `assetSalesHistoryRows` — mock-generated (10 rows)

#### 2. Asset Purchase History
Same structure as sales. Additional column: Representative Name.
**Data source:** `assetPurchaseHistoryRows` — mock-generated

#### 3. Loan Repayment History
Additional columns: Loan ID, Loan Value, Repaid Value.
**Data source:** `loanRepaymentHistoryRows` — mock-generated

#### 4. Loan Disbursement History
Additional columns: Loan ID, Loan Value, Disbursed Value.
**Data source:** `loanDisbursementHistoryRows` — mock-generated

#### 5. Customer Wallet Deposits
Standard columns. No loan-specific fields.
**Data source:** `customerWalletDepositRows` — mock-generated

#### 6. Interest Settlements
Additional columns: Loan ID, Loan Value (small amounts).
**Data source:** `interestSettlementRows` — mock-generated

Each tab has its own `PaymentsHistoryTable` component rendering rows from `paymentsHistoryByTab[tab]`.
